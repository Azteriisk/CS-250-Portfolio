# CS Portfolio Submission – Sprint Review and Retrospective

## Reflection Questions

**How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?**  
I interpret user needs by breaking them down into smaller, actionable requirements. Writing user stories helps me understand the perspective of the end user and gives me a concrete way to think about how the program should function. It makes the connection between what the user wants and how I will design the code.

**How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?**  
I approach development by planning small, achievable goals and building iteratively. Agile processes such as sprints, retrospectives, and daily standups help keep development focused while allowing flexibility when requirements change. I plan to keep using user stories, backlog grooming, and sprint reviews in my future work because they keep progress visible and help teams adapt quickly.

**What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?**  
Being a good team member means communicating openly, sharing progress, and helping remove obstacles for others. It’s about being reliable, listening to feedback, and keeping the team’s goals ahead of individual preferences. A good team member adapts to change, respects deadlines, and makes sure the work they deliver is high quality so others can build on it.
